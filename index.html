STRATEGIC PREMISE

"The studio's role is not to build, but to activate."

B2CREATE is not a creative agency in the conventional sense. It is a regional brand consultancy operating at institutional scale across Beirut, Cairo, KSA, and Oman. The studio's core idea reframes the agency-client relationship.

Month 1's job: install the premise. Establish institutional weight. Open the conversation with corporate decision-makers in MENA. Convert the studio's internal discipline (six values, six principles, five-stage process) into external trust signals.

The four weeks mirror the studio's own narrative arc: from dispersed to inevitable.
